### 💡 Data and AI Literacy Foundations — Learning Reflection

In the **Data and AI Literacy Foundations** course, I worked through core concepts spanning data analytics fundamentals, modern ways of working, Generative AI applications, structured problem-solving, and programmatic thinking.

* **Key Achievements:** I can now break down complex data problems logically, integrate AI into data practice, and approach analytical workflows with confidence.
* **Lessons Learned:** Mastering programmatic logic and AI prompting required shifting my problem-solving mindset—teaching me the value of structured, step-by-step reasoning over quick fixes.
* **Key Breakthrough:** Realizing how seamlessly AI tools and programming concepts connect to streamline analysis, turning abstract ideas into practical, everyday skills.
